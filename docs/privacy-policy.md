Thrive Internet Marketing Agency ("Thrive," "we," "us") provides the Thrive AI Visibility app, which runs AI search visibility scans of business websites from within AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude (the "App").

## What we collect

- **Website domain you submit for scanning.** This is the only required input. We fetch publicly available pages of that website to analyze them.
- **Optional context you choose to provide** (business description, service area, target customer) to improve scan accuracy.
- **Technical request data** (IP address of the requesting service, user agent, timestamps) used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
- **Marketing attribution tags** (e.g., that a scan originated from the App rather than our website).

We do **not** receive your ChatGPT or Claude account information, your conversation history, or any personal information beyond what you type into the scan inputs. Payment information is never collected — the App is free.

## How we use it

- To run the scan: we query third-party AI services (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Perplexity) with generic industry questions — never with your personal information — and analyze whether the scanned brand appears in their answers.
- To generate and host your scan report and PDF.
- To follow up about Thrive services: scans create a record in our CRM (Zoho) so our team can see who has used the tool. If you book a strategy call via the Calendly link, Calendly's own privacy policy applies to the information you give them.
- To prevent abuse (rate limiting by IP and domain).

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Scan queries are processed by our AI subprocessors (OpenAI, Google, Perplexity) as described above. Reports are hosted on Render. Scheduling is handled by Calendly. CRM records live in Zoho.

## Retention

Scan results are retained so reports remain accessible and so repeat scans of the same domain can be served from cache (currently ~30 days for cache; reports persist until deleted). You may request deletion of a scan or any associated data.
